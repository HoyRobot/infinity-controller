Built from current Pinfinity Web Bluetooth research and current TableBot protocol/firmware research. Version 3 uses the documented JOOLA frame, CRC-CCITT, FFE0/FEE7 service support, 20-byte BLE chunks, conservative inter-chunk delay, 0x05 info/idle preparation, 0x04 PRE, 0x01 standard PATTERN, 0x03 live STOP, and the TableBot community motor table.

First-use safety

Keep access to the robot's physical power switch during the first live test. This page is based on working community protocol research, but your exact Bluefy + iPhone + robot firmware combination is not proven until your first connection.
